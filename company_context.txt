## Product Overview

**Product Type:** Technology Implementation Studio / Service Business
**Primary Output:** Shipped technology products for clients
**Status:** Production (multiple case studies completed)

### One-Liner
Technology implementation studio that turns research into shipped products across AI, spatial computing, neurotech, and product development.

### Problem Statement
70-88% of digital transformations fail. Most failures happen because:
- Wrong problem — Technology chosen before constraint understood
- Wrong approach — AI treated as magic, not engineering
- Wrong scope — Ambition without path to execution
- Wrong finish line — Pilots that never become products

### Solution
1. Define the problem precisely
2. Determine if technology solves it
3. Build only what's needed
4. Ship working products, not decks

### Value Proposition
- Start with problem, not technology
- Multi-domain expertise (not just AI)
- Exits and operational experience behind them
- Global presence
- Ship products, not decks

## Core Workflows

### Workflow 1: Enterprise AI Implementation
1. Client has data/knowledge management problem
2. GameChangers researches, designs RAG/LLM architecture
3. Build production system with evaluation frameworks
4. Deploy to client's workflow tools
5. Measure outcomes against defined success criteria

### Workflow 2: Spatial Computing Experience
1. Client wants interactive/immersive experience
2. GameChangers translates creative vision to technical spec
3. Build VR/AR/Fortnite experience
4. Deploy with analytics
5. Measure engagement

### Workflow 3: Product Development / Pivot
1. Client has product opportunity or pivot need
2. Market research and analysis
3. Build working prototype (not wireframes)
4. Go-to-market framework
5. Test with real users

## Case Studies (Proof Points)

### 1. BrainV Technology (Neurotech)
- **Problem:** EEG hardware generating unusable data for spa practitioners
- **Solution:** Software layer translating raw EEG to actionable wellness metrics
- **Outcome:** Platform operational in premium spas, international expansion underway

### 2. Chinese Energy Company (Enterprise AI)
- **Problem:** BD teams drowning in research across scattered documents
- **Solution:** RAG system with natural language queries, source attribution
- **Outcome:** 60% reduction in research-to-discovery time

### 3. Beauty Product Company (Product Development)
- **Problem:** Physical product company exploring SAAS pivot
- **Solution:** Market analysis + working prototype (not wireframes)
- **Outcome:** Testing SAAS model with real customers, data-driven pivot decision

### 4. Grammy-Winning Artists (Gaming/Spatial)
- **Problem:** Commoditized music releases, need differentiated fan experiences
- **Solution:** Custom Fortnite experiences built around artist identities
- **Outcome:** Multiple projects shipped, millions of players reached

- Ship working products, not decks
- Functional technology in real world
- Stay engaged through deployment
- Measure outcomes against defined goals
